About

I am a graduate researcher at the Indian Institute of Technology Patna, working on blockchain and distributed systems security at the Security Lab. My research focuses on understanding how these systems behave under realistic adversarial conditions and developing practical approaches to evaluate and improve their security, trust, and reliability.

Previously, I completed my B.Tech in Computer Science (Honours) from KL University with a specialization in cybersecurity and blockchain technology, graduating with a CGPA of 9.26/10. My research journey began during my sophomore year, evolving from system vulnerability analysis into focused investigation of AI-driven security threats and cryptographic systems.
